# 游戏视觉资产全面重绘执行计划与提示词库 v1.9

> 项目：老街品牌局  
> 日期：2026-09-05  
> 状态：执行规格，替代 v1.8 中过于概括的“下一波改进”部分；v1.8 的审计结论继续保留  
> 核心目标：让每张图承担一个明确的经营判断，不再把参考图、氛围图、VI 展板和真实应用混为一谈

## 1. 本轮必须解决的问题

1. 运行时仍存在跨环节重复、同图换色和不同选择共用泛化图片的问题。
2. 用户提供的 VI 图片本应只是风格、材质和构图参考，其中若含其他品牌名称、LOGO、包装或人物，不得裁切、改色、压缩后直接进入游戏。
3. 店招、杯身、纸袋、头像和小票上的视觉系统目前是矩形卡片叠加，不符合真实表面透视、曲率、褶皱、遮挡、印刷和光影。
4. 当前应用种类过少，遗漏门外导视、菜单、取杯、封口、员工、会员、社交、合作供货、配送和 IP 功能物件等关键品牌触点。
5. 品牌名尚未确定时，图片和界面不得出现“你的品牌名”、伪品牌名、随机英文口号或模型生成的伪文字。
6. 简报图、行动图和结果图的镜头语言过于接近，玩家不能在一眼内判断当前环节究竟在研究顾客、产品、成本、识别、服务还是增长。

## 2. 可验收目标

- 12 个回合的简报图拥有 12 种明确的叙事焦点和至少 8 种不同镜头语言。
- 36 个阶段行动拥有 36 张语义唯一的特写，不再由 `research / test / quote` 三张通用图循环承担。
- 12 组结果图均由各自的批准母场景编辑出 `stable / strained / crisis`，状态差异必须是物件、队伍、动作和交付事实的变化，不能只是综合色调变化。
- 用户参考图与运行时正式图片之间不存在直接复制、裁切、重新编码或近似复刻。
- 至少覆盖 28 类真实品牌触点；其中游戏核心比较器先接入 10 类，其他触点按对应回合进入决策详情和结果反馈。
- 三条视觉路线使用同一组空白样机、同一机位、同一光线和同一材质，保证公平比较。
- 未命名阶段只显示空白载体或纯图形符号；命名完成后只由浏览器实时渲染玩家实际填写的名称。
- 手机端 360×800、390×844，平板 768×1024，桌面 1366×768 均能保留主体与关键证据。

## 3. 生产职责必须拆开

| 层                     | 负责内容                                                           | 禁止内容                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| ImageGen 照片层        | 门店、人物、动作、产品结构、空白包装、材料、光影、真实经营状态     | 中文品牌名、LOGO、第三方包装、VI 展板、UI 文案 |
| SVG/HTML/Canvas 品牌层 | 玩家实际品牌名、字标、符号、IP、色彩、组合规范、单色/缩小/遮挡测试 | 把完整卡片直接盖在照片上                       |
| UI 数据层              | 当前回合、玩家路线、已选触点、状态、alt 文本和分析指标             | 用图片颜色替代状态数据                         |

### 3.1 品牌名规则

- R01—R04：所有品牌承载面保持空白，不显示任何替代名称。
- R05 尚未完成命名时：允许展示 A/B/C 路线代号和纯图形，但代号只在界面文字层出现，不烘焙进图片。
- 玩家提交名称后：只渲染 `brandIdentity.brandName` 的真实值。
- 空值回退：显示纯符号或空白，不得回退为“你的品牌名”。
- 删除 `OLD STREET TEA BAR`、`CUP · LEAF · STREET`、`A WARM HELLO, WELL MADE` 等固定英文占位口号。

### 3.2 参考图使用规则

每次生成都要显式声明输入角色：

```text
Input images:
- Image 1..N: style / material / camera-structure references only; none is an edit target.
- Do not preserve or reproduce any existing brand name, logo, packaging artwork, slogan,
  character identity, exact layout or product silhouette from the references.
- Generate an original Tunxi Old Street tea-shop scene with new people, new objects and new graphics.
```

下列内容列入 OCR 与人工黑名单：用户参考图中出现的全部品牌名，以及“三叁堂”“茶余”“简山”“TEASOUL”“Moshoan”“你的品牌名”和任何随机英文品牌词。

## 4. 世界母版与统一输出规格

### 4.1 世界连续性

- 地点：安徽黄山屯溪老街的一间窄面宽、真实可经营的小型茶饮店。
- 建筑：石板路、白墙、深色木构、黑瓦檐口，避免影视城式夸张灯笼和符号堆砌。
- 室内：浅米石材柜台、深木收纳、少量竹与半透明纸材、真实制茶设备和清洗/库存痕迹。
- 固定人物：同一位小店经营者、同一位主店员；人物自然、不过度磨皮、不广告摆拍。
- 固定空间：同一门洞、柜台、出杯台、后架和门外视线关系。
- 色彩：宣纸白、墨黑、苔茶绿、砖铜、深木；危机状态也不能用满屏红色滤镜。

### 4.2 输出规格

| 类型             |           母版 |            运行时 | 构图安全区                       |
| ---------------- | -------------: | ----------------: | -------------------------------- |
| 简报、章节、结果 | 1600×900，16:9 | 1280×720 WebP/JPG | 主证据位于中央横向 70%、纵向 76% |
| 行动、决策、产品 |  1200×900，4:3 |      800×600 WebP | 单一主体占 45%—70%               |
| 触点样机         | 1600×1200，4:3 |     1000×750 WebP | 贴附面四角完整可见               |
| 透明 IP/物件     |      1600×1600 |  800×800 WebP/PNG | 四边至少 12% 透明留白            |
| LOGO/图形        |            SVG |               SVG | 24 px、单色、35% 遮挡均可测      |

生产图内不写 UI 文案；卡片标题、价格、提示和状态由 HTML 显示。每张图记录 `focalPoint`，禁止只依靠 `object-fit: cover` 猜测裁切。

## 5. 可直接复用的母提示词

### 5.1 场景母提示词 `BASE-SCENE`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: narrative game scene for a mobile-first business simulation
Primary request: an original, believable small tea-drink business scene in Tunxi Old Street,
Huangshan, Anhui, showing one precise business question through observable behavior
Scene/backdrop: the same narrow corner shop in every image; gray flagstone street, whitewashed
wall, dark timber frames and black-tile eaves outside; light beige stone counter, dark-wood storage,
real tea equipment, cleaning area and small-batch inventory inside
Recurring subjects: the same Chinese small-business operator and the same main staff member;
natural working posture, practical neutral clothing, realistic skin and fabric texture
Style/medium: photorealistic documentary editorial photography, restrained commercial polish,
real material wear, natural human expressions, subtle grain
Lighting/mood: soft daylight or motivated warm shop light; realistic contrast; no cinematic spectacle
Composition/framing: one clear focal action, readable at mobile size, important evidence kept in the
central safe area, no decorative split screen or collage
Constraints: all brand-bearing surfaces blank; no readable text, no pseudo-text, no logo, no slogan,
no watermark, no third-party brand, no copied packaging, no poster-style VI board
Avoid: generic luxury tea lounge, empty staged showroom, repeated front-facing group portrait,
oversaturated orange-and-teal grading, red crisis wash, fake ancient props, excessive lanterns,
floating UI panels, infographic arrows, extra fingers, malformed cups
```

### 5.2 行动特写母提示词 `BASE-ACTION`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: 4:3 action-card close-up
Primary request: show exactly one practical research, test, costing or operations action in the
same approved tea shop
Composition/framing: close-up, over-the-shoulder, top-down or detail shot as specified; one primary
hand-object interaction; the evidence must remain legible without any written label
Continuity: keep the approved operator, staff uniform, counter materials and light direction
Constraints: any notebook, receipt, calculator, screen or form uses blank blocks or unreadable marks;
no brand, no logo, no readable text, no watermark, no collage
Avoid: handshake stock photo, people pointing at floating charts, generic laptop meeting,
identical notebook in every action, decorative tea ceremony unrelated to the task
```

### 5.3 空白样机母提示词 `BASE-MOCKUP`

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: original blank physical touchpoint master for runtime brand compositing
Primary request: one realistic tea-shop touchpoint in the approved Tunxi Old Street shop world
Style/medium: photorealistic product and environmental photography
Composition/framing: slight three-quarter or specified camera; the full printable surface and all
four surface corners visible; generous surrounding context; no floating product
Materials/textures: real paper fibers, cup curvature, fabric weave, wood grain, creases, edge wear,
soft contact shadows and believable highlights
Constraints: the application surface is completely blank; no logo, no text, no pseudo-text,
no watermark, no third-party object; preserve a clean maskable surface for later SVG/Canvas placement
Avoid: isolated white-background catalog render unless specified, fake glossy plastic,
front-facing sticker pasted over the object, multiple competing products
```

### 5.4 统一状态编辑母提示词 `BASE-RESULT-EDIT`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Input images: Image 1 is the approved base scene for this exact round and is the edit target
Primary request: change only the observable operating state specified below
Invariants: preserve camera position, crop, lens, shop geometry, recurring people, facial identity,
clothing, time of day, light direction, selected route props and all blank brand surfaces
Constraints: state difference must be visible through customer behavior, queue, work surface,
inventory, handoff and object placement; do not change overall color grade; no text, logo or watermark
```

### 6.2 四张章节成长图

四张必须由同一个 16:9 店铺母场景连续编辑：

- C1 顾客：入口有少量明确的优先顾客，菜单与取杯节奏仍简陋。

## 7. 十二回合简报图：逐轮镜头与完整追加提示词

以下提示词均接在 `BASE-SCENE` 后。每轮镜头不得被其他回合复用。

### R01 先别急着做 Logo — 街角观察广角

```text
Early morning street-level 28mm establishing shot outside the same blank corner shop. A nearby
resident carrying breakfast, a visitor with a camera and a commuter with a bicycle cross the frame
at different speeds. The operator stands just inside the doorway with a small tally counter,
watching who slows down and who actually enters. Keep the blank storefront sign visible but not
dominant. The image must communicate customer segmentation, not shop decoration.
```

### R02 一句话要先对谁说 — 双焦点柜台中景

```text
Afternoon 50mm medium shot from beside the counter. In the foreground, an office worker checks the
time and reaches toward the quick pickup area; in the middle ground, a visitor examines loose tea
leaves and asks a question with curious posture. The operator is caught between the two needs beside
one blank menu plane. Use two depth planes, not a split screen. The image must communicate that two
audiences need different entry points.
```

### R03 产品不是品牌的配饰 — 制作台侧面特写

```text
Side-on 70mm close shot of the production bench. Three same-size drinks require visibly different
numbers of tools and steps: a consistent everyday tea, a layered photo-oriented drink and a
tea-forward local-ingredient drink. The staff member is mid-preparation while order slips begin to
occupy the edge. Show real tea liquid, condensation, measuring tools and workflow cost; no fantasy
dessert styling.
```

### R04 价格不是最后才填的数字 — 包装成本俯拍

```text
True top-down 90-degree still-life of one daily cup, one compact gift box, one refill container,
three package sizes and one tea-and-snack carrier, all blank. Surround them with flat paper stock,
internal supports, carton volume, a scale and a calculator with an unreadable display. One operator
hand compares the packaging weight against the costing sheet. The focal evidence is structure,
storage and per-unit burden, not decorative branding.
```

### R05 名字、LOGO 和 IP 各自要做什么 — 缩放测试微距

```text
Controlled 85mm design-review close-up, not a mood board. The same three original identity structures
are tested at three physical scales: a narrow blank storefront strip, a small circular phone-avatar
frame and a tiny monochrome stamp area. The designer uses calipers and a print loupe while the
operator views the storefront sample from farther back. All exact graphics are supplied later by
SVG; image areas remain neutral and blank. Show system usability, not pretty sketches.
```

### R06 开业第一周，品牌要出现在哪里 — 顾客路径主观镜头

```text
35mm customer-eye point-of-view from the street into the shop, following one continuous path:
blank fascia and blade-sign planes at the entrance, a cup sleeve at the order point, a small pickup
marker, and a phone recording the preparation process farther inside. The operator studies which
one or two touchpoints actually receive attention. Keep some candidate touchpoints intentionally
unfinished to show a constrained opening budget.
```

### R07 服务也是品牌的一部分 — 双手交接与动线特写

```text
50mm counter-height close shot focused on a cup handoff: one staff hand checks the cup marker before
passing it to a customer, while a second staff member and the pickup route remain visible in the
background. Another customer is waiting and checking the time. Show ordering, waiting, pickup and
departure as a practical flow through hands and positions, not through a generic crowded interior.
```

### R08 设计进入现实 — 同规格触点实验台

```text
Straight-on controlled comparison scene with exactly five identical blank touchpoints: narrow
storefront sign model, curved cup sleeve, creased paper bag, circular phone avatar and monochrome
receipt header. The operator and designer test viewing distance, 24-pixel scale, grayscale and
partial hand occlusion. Every surface is blank for exact SVG placement. Neutral light, equal size,
equal angle and equal quality; no VI presentation board.
```

### R09 顾客愿意替你说什么 — 肩后真实分享特写

```text
Over-the-shoulder 55mm shot from behind a returning customer holding a phone, but the screen contains
only an unreadable neutral post skeleton and one blank image tile. Across the table, a visitor tells
a story about the tea ingredient while the staff member sorts a small stack of feedback cards.
Show three forms of advocacy—sharing, storytelling and feedback—in one natural interaction without
floating social-media graphics or influencer posing.
```

### R10 增长要不要跨出这条街 — 后场交付剖面

```text
40mm diagonal view from the cramped back room toward the working counter. In the foreground are one
hotel-supply crate, one blank collaboration gift-box prototype, one insulated delivery bag and a
small second-window scale model; in the background the same two-person team still serves the shop.
The narrow aisle and limited storage make capacity visible. Focus on channel-specific delivery
requirements rather than a generic meeting table.
```

### R11 火了以后全是事 — 队伍压缩长焦

```text
85mm compressed view from inside the shop toward the entrance. A real queue fills the doorway;
finished cups, order markers and delivery bags accumulate in separate layers. A regular customer
cannot easily reach the pickup point while the staff remain focused on production. Show popularity
and operational pressure simultaneously, without angry mob caricature or red disaster lighting.
```

### R12 一年以后，品牌要留下什么 — 年度档案桌近景

```text
50mm dusk still-life with the operator standing behind one long review table. The foreground holds
one mature product, used packaging samples, a repaired service tool, blank customer feedback cards,
one small supply box, inventory notes and a functional IP object. The same shop is visible beyond,
quietly operating at night. Show accumulated relationships and operating knowledge, not a trophy,
portfolio board or sentimental group portrait.
```

### 11.2 接入规则

- 店招、菜单、纸袋、礼盒：保存四点贴附多边形、裁切路径、纸面纹理混合和前景遮挡蒙版。

- 围裙：使用布料褶皱蒙版与 `multiply/soft-light` 纹理，不允许矩形白底。

## 15. 自动与人工验收门

### 15.1 自动检查

- 运行时映射的每个栅格路径都必须出现在正式 manifest；manifest 也不得漏掉运行时引用。
- SHA-256 完全重复为零；跨用途感知哈希过近必须人工解释，同一结果母图的三态编辑除外。
- 与 `docs/visual-references/` 的感知哈希/SSIM 近似命中直接阻断发布。
- OCR 命中黑名单、随机英文、第三方品牌、可读伪菜单或水印直接阻断。
- 简报/结果尺寸和比例统一；竖长图不得进入横版场景映射。
- 所有 36 个行动 ID 必须有独立路径；测试不只检查路径字符串不同，还检查文件内容不同。
- `rg "你的品牌名|OLD STREET TEA BAR|CUP · LEAF · STREET|A WARM HELLO"` 在运行时源码与正式资产中无命中。

### 15.2 人工检查

每张图由两名未看标题的检查者回答：

1. 这张图发生的是“观察、测试、成本、服务、识别、传播、交付”中的哪一类？
2. 主证据在手机缩略图中是否仍可见？
3. 是否像真实小店会发生的动作，而不是摆拍、VI 展板或电商海报？
4. 是否出现其他品牌、伪文字、过度仿古、人物异常或无关装饰？
5. 三态之间能否用一句“发生了什么变化”解释，而不是只说颜色变了？

简报与行动图的环节识别率至少 80%；核心 R04/R05/R07/R08 样片必须达到 100% 后才批量扩展。

### 15.3 UI 验收

- 360×800 与 390×844：主体不被裁掉，图片不挤压选择按钮，触控目标保持可用。
- 768×1024：详情图与解释文字层级清楚。
- 1366×768：不因宽屏把人物、杯子或贴附面裁到边缘。
- 加载失败、弱网和未命名状态均有无文字污染的占位方案。
- 动效减弱模式不影响结果事实的理解。
