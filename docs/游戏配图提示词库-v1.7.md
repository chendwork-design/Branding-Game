# 老街品牌局：游戏配图提示词库 v1.7

## 0. 使用说明

本文件是《游戏配图生产计划 v1.6》的生产级提示词补充，并对其中不够严谨的部分做出修正。它不是“风格形容词合集”，而是每张图可以直接执行、检查和迭代的生产规格。

默认使用内置 ImageGen。用户提供的图片均为**参考图**，不是编辑目标；正式状态变体以前一步批准的母图为**编辑目标**。每次调用最多使用 4 张真正相关的参考图，避免把十几张风格不同的案例同时塞入一次生成。

所有提示词都遵守以下规则：

- 图片模型只生成空间、人物、产品、材质和经营动作；
- 玩家品牌名、准确中文、LOGO、IP 主图形、菜单和 UI 由 SVG/HTML 制作；
- 正式资产不能复制参考图中的品牌、人物身份、文字、版式、水印或独特图形；
- 一张图只表达一个主要问题；如果需要同时表达流程，拆成多张再由前端组合；
- 新资产先生成母版，状态变化使用编辑，不能每张重新生成导致人物和店铺漂移。

### 1.2 从游戏体验看，计划为什么成立

| 设计层            | 图片承担的作用                                                                         | 控制风险的方法                                                       |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mechanics 机制    | R03/R04/R05/R08/R12 的图片提供真实可比较的信息；结果状态由规则引擎决定，图片不改变数值 | 同机位、同完成度；图片只显示规则已经算出的状态，不能暗中给某方案加分 |
| Dynamics 动态     | 玩家会逐步形成“先观察—比较约束—做选择—看现场能否接住”的习惯                            | 简报保持中立；结果紧跟选择；章节复盘复用同一门店显示累积变化         |
| Aesthetics 体验   | 老街与真实门店提供代入感，清楚的状态变化提供紧张和成就感                               | 不把失败画成灾难，不把成功画成庆功；用日常经营差异维持可信度         |
| Autonomy 自主     | 三套视觉方向和多种产品/包装路线都可以成立                                              | 所有方案使用同等摄影质量，避免“最漂亮的图就是标准答案”               |
| Competence 胜任感 | 玩家能从结果图读出自己是否开始理解客群、交付和视觉触点                                 | 三档状态必须两秒可辨；章节成长必须能看见系统逐步成形                 |
| Flow 节奏         | 每轮只出现一张简报和一张结果主图，专业比较集中在少数关键回合                           | 不给每个文字选项配图，不增加相册、轮播和独立图片页                   |

### 1.3 仍需在首批小样中验证的风险

1. **写实一致性风险**：人物换脸、门店结构漂移会立即破坏沉浸。解决办法是先批准 4 个锚点，并只用批准母图做状态编辑。
2. **结果图过于通用**：同一状态图服务同回合多个选项，可能不足以解释具体因果。解决办法是把具体产品/包装/视觉缩略图放在结果标题附近，并用一行选择专属因果短句补齐；不强行在照片里塞抽象符号。
3. **视觉方案质量偏置**：如果 IP 方案更可爱、符号方案更醒目，学生可能只按画面吸引力选择。解决办法是五触点同底图、同尺寸、先黑白后配色，并在测试中隐藏方案名称进行盲选。
4. **12 轮重复疲劳**：所有画面如果都用同一广角柜台会缺乏节奏。解决办法是不同回合使用不同功能机位，但同一回合三档状态必须锁死机位。
5. **生产体量风险**：75 张源图不适合一次性批量生成。解决办法是先通过 22 项发布门，再按回合生产；任何锚点失败都停止扩散。
6. **移动端性能风险**：源图数量多不等于一次加载多。必须按回合懒加载、只预取下一张，并用真实网络面板验证 650 KB 页面预算。

这六项没有通过首批发布门前，计划仍处于“可执行但未获视觉验证”的状态，不能因为提示词写得详细就宣称美术方向已经完成。

## 3. 全局锁定词

### 3.1 写实经营场景锁定词

以下内容附在所有引导、简报、结果和章节场景后：

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: mobile-first narrative management game scene.
World lock: a believable contemporary tea-drink shop in a real Tunxi Old Street commercial environment in Huangshan, Anhui. The street has gray stone paving, white plaster walls, dark tiled roofs, narrow storefronts and present-day pedestrians in ordinary modern clothes. This is not a historical reenactment.
Shop lock: a narrow 3.2-meter-wide, about 8-meter-deep shop; one 0.85-meter-high pale rough-stone service counter; at least 1.1 meters of staff passage; visible but compact tea-brewing, sealing, washing and storage areas. Materials are warm translucent paper, light oak, slender bamboo details and dark tea-brown metal. The shop is attractive but operationally believable and slightly used.
Photography: photorealistic Chinese editorial documentary photography, natural skin and fabric texture, subtle film grain, realistic depth of field, no beauty-ad retouching. Camera height 1.50–1.60 meters unless otherwise specified. Do not use an ultra-wide distorted interior.
Responsive composition: keep the main human action, cups and operational clue within the central 60% of the frame. Keep the top 12% and bottom 10% free of irreplaceable details. Allow both 4:3 mobile and 3:2 desktop crops.
Continuity: use the approved shop anchor, operator anchor and staff anchor. Preserve faces, age, hair, apron, body proportions, counter geometry and equipment locations. New customers may vary.
Text handling: all signs, menu boards, cup labels, packaging panels, receipts and phone screens are blank or softly out of focus. No readable Chinese, no random letters, no generated logo, no brand name, no UI.
Avoid: costume drama, ancient-market fantasy, theme park, luxury-mall showroom, empty architectural render, cyberpunk, exaggerated ink-painting filter, plastic skin, idol advertising pose, copied real brand, watermark, pseudo-Chinese characters, distorted hands, extra fingers, floating cups, impossible equipment, blocked staff passage, excessive props, celebratory confetti.
```

### 3.2 产品和包装公平比较锁定词

```text
Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: fair-comparison decision image for a mobile management game.
Fairness lock: use exactly the same camera, focal length, background, object scale, crop, shadow softness, blank brand zone and completion quality for every option in the set. Do not give one option a richer environment, brighter light, more props or a more attractive hand model.
Set: warm off-white rough stone tabletop, soft translucent-paper background, restrained light-oak edge, neutral daylight from upper left, soft fill from front. No dramatic spotlight.
Materials: realistic small-batch paper, molded pulp, food-safe plastic, glass or coated card as appropriate; visible seams, folds, closures, condensation and weight.
Text handling: all brand and information panels remain blank. No readable Chinese, letters, numbers, logos, barcodes or watermark.
Composition: product centered in the same 70% scale envelope; generous but equal margins; 50 mm product-photography perspective; no wide-angle distortion.
Avoid: luxury perfume styling, fantasy tea ingredients, excessive flowers, impossible packaging, zero-gravity props, giant garnish, copied brand design, fake text.
```

### 3.3 状态编辑锁定词

三档状态必须以批准的回合母图为编辑目标，不得重新生成。

#### `stable`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Input image: Image 1 is the approved base scene and the only edit target.
Primary request: change only the operational outcome to a stable, believable state. Preserve every recurring person's identity, face, hair, clothing, body proportions and position range; preserve camera, crop, architecture, counter, equipment, selected product/packaging/route props, time of day and lighting direction.
Visible changes: manageable queue of 2–4 people where relevant; clear work surface; enough ingredients and cups; correct handoff; staff can still look up and explain; customers understand where to order and collect. Success is ordinary and repeatable, not celebratory.
Do not add: smiles to everyone, cheering, trophies, confetti, luxury decoration or extra staff.
```

#### `strained`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Input image: Image 1 is the approved base scene and the only edit target.
Primary request: change only the operational outcome to a pressured but still functioning state. Preserve identities, camera, geometry, selected route props, product type, time and lighting.
Visible changes: queue of about 5–7 people where relevant; 5–8 order slips or equivalent workload clues; work surface about 65% occupied; one ingredient or packaging stack visibly running low; staff concentrate and stop explaining; one customer checks the time or hesitates. No shouting, crying or disaster.
```

#### `crisis`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Input image: Image 1 is the approved base scene and the only edit target.
Primary request: change only the operational outcome to a clear promise-delivery mismatch while the shop is still open. Preserve identities, camera, architecture, selected route props, product type, time and lighting.
Visible changes: queue or pickup flow becomes unclear; one wrong or missing package is visible; a staff member searches for supplies or rechecks an order; one customer holds the wrong cup or asks for clarification; inventory shortage or blocked work surface is visible. Use restrained real-world awkwardness, not slapstick.
Do not add: bankruptcy signs, fire, broken machinery, injured people, panic, screaming, tears, exaggerated mess or moral judgment.
```

## 4. 四张内部锚点提示词

锚点先批准，后续所有场景才能开始。每个锚点只选 2–4 张参考图。

### W00-E：老街门店外部锚点

**参考图**：`EX-DAY-01`（地点）、`EX-CROWD-04`（街道密度）、`INT-WIDE-02`（店铺入口尺度）。

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: world-location anchor, not a final game scene.
Primary request: establish one original narrow tea-drink storefront on Tunxi Old Street that can remain visually consistent through the entire game.
Scene: late morning on a real present-day old street. Gray stone paving extends diagonally into depth. White plaster and dark timber buildings line both sides. The game shop occupies a modest 3.2-meter-wide frontage, not a corner flagship. Its entrance is open, with one blank horizontal sign panel, one blank vertical menu panel and a glimpse of a pale stone counter inside.
Composition: eye-level at 1.58 meters, 32–35 mm lens, camera about 7 meters from the facade at a slight 15-degree angle. Show enough neighboring street context to identify the location, but keep the blank shop frontage as the clear focal point. No dominant gate or tourist monument in the center.
People: 5–8 ordinary present-day pedestrians distributed naturally; no one looking at camera; no recurring main characters yet.
Lighting: clear but soft late-morning daylight, realistic shadow direction, no orange cinematic grade.
Constraints: create an original storefront; do not reproduce any readable sign, exact shop, logo or architectural ornament from the references. Apply the global scene lock.
```

### W00-I：门店内部锚点

**参考图**：`INT-WIDE-02`（入口与尺度）、`INT-MAT-04`（材料）、`INT-COUNTER-07`（柜台层次）、`OPS-BACKBAR-08`（真实设备密度）。

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: fixed interior anchor and later chapter-growth edit target.
Primary request: create the canonical interior of the same narrow tea-drink shop, prepared for trial opening but not yet branded.
Geometry: approximately 3.2 meters wide and 8 meters deep. Camera stands just inside the entrance at 1.55 meters high, using a restrained 26–28 mm lens. A pale rough-stone service counter begins on the right and leads toward the back; a 1.1-meter staff passage remains clear. Behind it are a tea brewer, induction kettle, sealing machine, under-counter refrigerator, sink, cup rack and two modest ingredient shelves. A small packaging table and closed storage occupy the rear left. Do not invent a full restaurant kitchen.
Materials: warm off-white translucent ceiling panels, light oak, sparse bamboo members, dark tea-brown metal, textured pale stone and gray floor tile. Slight everyday wear, no pristine 3D-render surface.
Brand zones: one blank sign fascia visible through the entrance, one blank menu board, blank cup sleeves and bags. No generated text.
Mood: calm late morning before opening, daylight from the street mixed with warm paper light. No people blocking the geometry.
Constraints: operational clearances must be believable; preserve this layout in all later interior scenes. Apply the global scene lock.
```

### W00-O：经营者角色锚点

**参考图**：`OPS-CASHIER-07`（职业站姿）、`OPS-SERVICE-11`（克制服务动作）。两张只作动作参考，不保留人物身份。

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: recurring character anchor.
Primary request: an original Chinese young brand operator in their early twenties who looks capable but inexperienced, suitable for a realistic course-management game.
Appearance: ordinary approachable Chinese face, natural skin texture, short dark hair or neatly tied hair, no celebrity resemblance. Dark muted-olive work apron over a warm-gray cotton shirt, straight dark trousers and practical shoes. One simple notebook and a shop key are the only props.
Pose: one single three-quarter full-body view, standing naturally and looking slightly off camera as if checking the shop rather than posing. The face must remain large and clear enough to reuse as an identity reference; both hands must be visible and natural. Do not create a multi-view collage.
Backdrop: plain warm neutral area inside the approved shop, softly out of focus.
Lighting: soft window light; restrained color; no beauty retouching.
Constraints: create a new identity; preserve face, hair, age, height and apron silhouette in later scenes. No makeup-ad look, fashion pose, oversized eyes, logo or text.
```

### W00-S：店员角色锚点

**参考图**：`STAFF-POSE-01`（持杯动作）、`OPS-CASHIER-07`（收银动作）、`OPS-BACKBAR-08`（工作环境）。均只作动作参考。

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: recurring staff character anchor.
Primary request: an original Chinese tea-shop staff member in their early twenties, friendly and practical rather than promotional.

Backdrop: approved shop counter and equipment softly visible.
Constraints: do not copy reference faces; preserve identity and uniform silhouette across scenes; anatomically plausible fingers and cup grip; no text or logo.
```

## 5. 新手引导提示词

### `onboarding-place`

**参考图**：W00-E（编辑/一致性参考）、W00-O（角色参考）、`EX-DAY-01`（客流行为）。

```text
Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: onboarding screen 1, “your situation”.
Primary request: show the approved operator unlocking the approved empty storefront for the first time while three different kinds of potential customers pass through the real old street.
Action: the operator stands just inside the open door holding the key and a small notebook. A nearby resident carrying a grocery bag walks past without stopping; a young visitor slows and looks into the blank shop; a commuter with a bicycle moves through the middle distance. Unopened supply boxes are visible just inside, but do not block the entrance.
Composition: same exterior facade as W00-E, eye-level 35 mm, operator on one third, street depth visible. The three customer behaviors must be readable without labels and none should appear obviously “correct”.
Mood: early morning, slightly uncertain but promising.
Constraints: preserve storefront and operator identity; all signs blank; apply the global scene lock.
```

每回合先生成 `stable` 版本并批准；`stable` 同时作为该回合的编辑母图，再分别应用第 3.3 节的 `strained` 和 `crisis` 编辑词。这样每回合仍只有三张发行图，不额外产生 12 张未计数的中性母图。

### 7.0 `stable` 母图生成模板

将下表对应回合的信息填入模板，并附加第 3.1 节全局锁定词：

```text

Asset type: round-result stable master and edit target for later strained/crisis variants.
Input images: Image 1 is the approved shop exterior or interior anchor; Image 2 is the approved operator anchor when visible; Image 3 is the approved staff anchor when visible; Image 4, if used, is one action/material reference only. These are reference images, not sources to copy.
Primary request: show the believable post-decision operating result for round <ROUND_ID> in a stable but ordinary state. The scene must visually answer the round's operational question without announcing a moral winner.
Round-specific camera: <FIXED CAMERA FROM TABLE>.
Must show: <REQUIRED INFORMATION FROM TABLE>.
```

## 11. 三套 SVG 视觉系统建设 brief

这些不是生图任务，不应交给图片模型生成最终标志。

### A `v-line`：一笔成街

- 先以玩家品牌名的系统字体做清晰横向字标；没有玩家名时使用“品牌名”占位文本，只存在于 HTML。
- 辅助线从徽派屋檐的折线节奏与街巷延伸感提炼，不描摹具体建筑。
- 主标志在 24 px 仍保留一条可识别折线；单色、反白均可用。
- 不能仿书法题字，不能复刻参考案例中的中文结构。

### B `v-symbol`：山影入杯

- 以“杯口椭圆 + 屋檐折线 + 一片茶叶负形”构成单一封闭轮廓。
- 先完成黑白，确保遮挡 35% 后仍能判断轮廓；再加入黄绿色。
- 不使用常见“三座山 + 水波 + 印章”组合，不画现成茶壶。

### C `v-hand`：茶友阿街

- 核心轮廓由杯套形身体、茶叶形侧耳/帽檐和一条蒸汽动作线构成。
- 只制作三种表情：欢迎、提醒、忙碌；两种动作：指向取杯、抱住回收杯套。
- 角色必须与清晰字标锁定使用，头像可单独使用头部轮廓。
- 不做儿童大眼、拟人茶宠、盲盒站姿或十几套换装。

## 14. 生成、检查与返工规则

### 14.1 每张图的第一次验收

1. **语义**：不看标题，能否说出这张图在讲什么经营问题？
2. **中立**：简报图是否无意中把某个选项拍得更高级、更明亮或更成功？
3. **因果**：结果图是否能看出稳定、吃力或明显失配，而不是只换了色调？
4. **现实**：设备、操作、队伍、包装体积和人物动作是否可发生？
5. **连续**：同一店、主角和店员有没有变脸、变年龄、变布局？
6. **移动端**：390 × 844 裁切后，关键动作是否仍在中心区域？
7. **合规**：是否存在伪中文、真实品牌、水印、复制的标志或可识别人物？

任一项失败，该图不能以“整体还可以”为理由通过。
