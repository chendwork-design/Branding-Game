# 老街品牌局：视觉资产参考与提示词修订 v1.5

> **版本说明（2026-08-30）**：用户后续补充的老街外景、经营现场和品牌案例已经归档。本文第 10 节“仍需用户提供的参考图”及第 11 节旧执行顺序不再适用；缺失参考由开发方自行补齐，完整资产范围、接入结构、执行顺序和验收门以 `docs/游戏配图生产计划-v1.6.md` 为准。本文第 1–9 节仅保留为早期场景意图记录；可直接生产的参考图调用、镜头、空间尺寸、动作和状态编辑词以 `docs/游戏配图提示词库-v1.7.md` 为准。

## 1. 修订结论

本版停止使用“2.5D 平面插画、几何建筑占位、轻微木刻纹理”作为正式场景图方向。用户提供的参考图指向的是更成熟的视觉语言：

- 现实可信的当代中国精品茶饮空间；
- 宣纸、竹、浅木、粗粝石材、深茶褐金属与暖白透光膜形成的材料系统；
- 暖色漫射光、克制陈列和真实零售尺度；
- 奶油白、茶褐、墨黑、灰绿和少量黄绿色点缀；
- 图片负责空间、人物、产品和经营现场，品牌名、LOGO、菜单文字和 UI 由 HTML/SVG 叠加；
- 场景图采用写实商业摄影与纪实编辑摄影之间的质感，不做卡通、游戏概念草图或旅游纪念品拼贴。

正式资产分为两类，不再要求所有图片套用同一种画法：

1. **剧情经营场景**：写实编辑摄影，强调真实人物、空间和经营后果。
2. **产品与视觉比较**：高保真产品样机摄影，严格同机位、同背景、同完成度，避免图片质量替玩家做决定。

## 3. 统一生产规则

### 3.1 剧情经营场景母提示词

以下段落附在所有新手引导、简报、结果和章节成长图之后：

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: mobile-first narrative management game scene. Input images: Images 02–07 are references for contemporary Chinese tea-shop spatial scale, warm translucent-paper lighting, bamboo, light wood and rough pale stone; Images 08–10 are references only for restrained cream, tea-brown, ink-black and muted olive color relationships; do not reproduce any existing brand, logo, text or exact layout. A believable contemporary tea-drink shop near Tunxi Old Street, not an ancient costume set and not a luxury mall showroom. Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography with natural human proportions, real skin and fabric texture, practical counter equipment, slight everyday wear and believable operational detail. Warm diffused daylight or warm paper-lantern light, restrained contrast, natural depth of field, no heavy beauty retouching. Main action stays inside the central 60% safe area; leave the top 12% and bottom 10% free of essential details for responsive 4:3 and 16:9 crops. No readable text, no generated Chinese characters, no brand name, no logo, no UI, no infographic, no watermark.

### 3.2 人物一致性补充词

> Keep the same two recurring characters from the approved character anchors: a Chinese brand operator in their early twenties wearing a dark muted-green work apron, and a Chinese tea-shop staff member in their early twenties wearing a tea-brown or black apron. Preserve facial structure, hair, height relationship, clothing silhouette and age across scenes. Expressions are subtle and situational. Hands must be anatomically plausible and interact naturally with cups, order slips and equipment.

### 3.3 产品与品牌触点母提示词

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: fair-comparison game decision asset. Input images: Images 08–10 are references for tactile paper, tea photography, muted olive, cream and tea-brown palette, generous negative space and a coherent family of touchpoints; do not reproduce their brand, wording, logo or exact composition. High-fidelity product and packaging photography on the same warm neutral set, same camera angle, same focal length, same scale and same completion quality across every option. Realistic small-batch materials, believable folds, seams, cup lids, paper grain and production constraints. All brand zones remain blank and clean for later SVG/HTML overlays. No readable text, no logo, no watermark, no decorative pseudo-Chinese characters.

### 3.4 统一避免项

> Avoid: flat vector illustration, childish game art, toy-like buildings, generic ancient-China scenery, costume drama, tourist-souvenir collage, cyberpunk, exaggerated ink painting, fantasy objects, mall luxury showroom, empty architectural rendering, plastic skin, idol portrait retouching, Korean beauty-ad look, impossible equipment, distorted hands, duplicate people, fake Chinese text, random English, copied brand identity, watermark, excessive props, floating UI, arrows, charts.

### 3.5 输出与裁切

- 场景母版：1536 × 1024，3:2；适配 4:3 与 16:9 裁切。
- 角色锚点：1024 × 1536，竖版半身和全身各一张。
- 产品比较：1536 × 1024 或 2048 × 2048；同一组保持完全一致的机位和光线。
- 关键人物、杯子、包装和操作台不得贴近画面边缘。
- 图片内不生成品牌名。玩家填写的品牌名由界面实时叠加。

### W00-A 门店空间锚点

> Primary request: a fixed establishing view of a newly leased narrow tea-drink shop near Tunxi Old Street, combining a modest street-facing entrance with a visible compact service counter. Scene/backdrop: contemporary renovation inside a real old-street building; warm translucent-paper ceiling and wall panels, light oak, slender bamboo details, rough off-white stone counter, dark tea-brown metal lines, stone paving visible outside. Subject: the shop is prepared for trial operation but still restrained—one practical drink station, tea-sample shelf, small packaging table and limited storage; no finished brand sign. Style/medium: photorealistic architectural editorial photography, lived-in retail realism rather than a pristine render. Composition/framing: eye-level wide shot, approximately 28–35 mm lens, fixed camera position to be reused in later chapter images. Lighting/mood: warm diffuse late-morning light, calm and promising. Constraints: preserve realistic equipment clearances and narrow shop proportions; no people blocking the main spatial structure; no readable signage; append the narrative master prompt and avoid list.

### W00-B 主角角色锚点

> Primary request: original Chinese young brand operator, early twenties, gender presentation neutral, short dark hair, ordinary approachable face, dark muted-green apron over practical neutral clothing, holding a notebook and shop key rather than posing like a fashion model. Style/medium: photorealistic editorial character reference, real skin texture and natural posture. Composition/framing: one full-body view and one waist-up view on a plain warm neutral shop backdrop. Lighting/mood: soft window light, thoughtful and alert. Constraints: create a new identity; do not copy any person from the reference images; no beauty-filter skin, no exaggerated eyes, no brand text.

### W00-C 店员角色锚点

> Input images: Image 01 is pose and occupational-action reference only. Primary request: original Chinese tea-shop staff member in their early twenties, practical ponytail or short tied-back hair, white or warm-gray shirt with a black or tea-brown apron and a plain cream cap, naturally holding one finished tea drink at the counter. Expression: friendly but professional, a small believable smile rather than an advertisement grin. Style/medium: photorealistic editorial workplace portrait with real skin and fabric texture. Composition/framing: waist-up, eye-level, enough room around hands and cup for later crops. Constraints: generate a different person; do not preserve or imitate Image 01's face; reduce retouching; cup has a blank label area and no logo.

## 5. 新手引导提示词

每张都使用 W00-A/B/C 作为一致性参考，并附剧情经营场景母提示词。

### `onboarding-place`

> Early morning outside the same narrow Tunxi Old Street tea shop. The brand operator stands at the newly opened entrance holding the key while a nearby resident walks briskly past, a visitor pauses to look into the shop, and a commuter moves through the street. The shop is only partly prepared, with a blank sign panel and visible unopened supply boxes. The emotional beat is “there are many possible customers, but the direction is not decided.” Wide eye-level establishing shot; realistic old-street scale; no readable shop signs.

### `onboarding-goal`

> Inside the same shop during a modest trial opening: one customer understands the offer and receives a drink, the staff member completes the drink smoothly, and the same restrained blank visual zones repeat naturally on the sign panel, cup sleeve and takeaway bag. Show these three layers working together in one believable moment, not as a split-screen, trophy or infographic. Medium-wide fixed shop angle, warm daylight, calm sense of a system beginning to work.

### `onboarding-operation`

> One continuous scene in the same shop: in the foreground the operator observes a real clue on the counter and customer behavior; in the middle ground two practical options are represented by two different physical setups; in the background the staff and customer experience the consequence of the chosen setup. Express observation, decision and consequence through depth and staging only—no arrows, labels, UI or floating panels.

## 7. 结果图制作提示词

结果图不应每次从头生成。每回合先确定一个固定母场景，再以母场景为编辑目标生成三档状态，必须保持机位、人物身份、服装、店铺结构、主要物件位置和时间段不变。

### 7.1 三档状态编辑词

#### `stable`

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Input images: Image 1 is the approved round base scene. Change only operational state and character reactions: keep the same camera, shop geometry, recurring people, clothing, lighting direction and selected route props. The work surface is controlled, the queue is manageable, customers complete the purchase naturally, staff still have attention for handoff and explanation, and inventory appears sufficient. Warm but ordinary success; no celebration pose, confetti or trophy.

#### `strained`

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Input images: Image 1 is the approved round base scene. Preserve camera, identity, shop geometry, selected route props and time of day. Add believable operational pressure only: more order slips, slightly longer wait, partially crowded work surface, focused and tense staff, one customer beginning to hesitate, and limited stock becoming visible. The shop is still functioning; no disaster, crying or slapstick collapse.

#### `failed`

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Input images: Image 1 is the approved round base scene. Preserve camera, identity, shop geometry, selected route props and time of day. Show a clear mismatch between promise and delivery: attention or photo-taking may be high, but cup handoff, stock, order accuracy or customer understanding is visibly confused. Use restrained realistic humor—wrongly placed packaging, an awkward queue or staff searching for supplies—without bankruptcy, fire, broken equipment or exaggerated emotional breakdown.

## 8. 产品、包装、LOGO 与 IP 决策资产提示词

### 8.1 R03 五类产品方案

统一基础：

> Five separate product photographs on the same light-stone and warm-paper set, same transparent cup proportions, same camera, same fill level and same blank label zone. Real tea ingredients, believable small-shop production, no brand text. Append the product/touchpoint master prompt.

各方案只改变以下变量：

- 稳定基础款：一层茶色和一层轻奶或清茶，装饰极少，杯间一致性高；
- 复杂拍照款：三层可见结构、额外顶饰和两件额外工具，视觉突出但步骤明显增加；
- 本地茶材款：茶汤和真实茶叶成为主角，呈现黄山茶材质感，不堆砌山水图案；
- 季节仪式款：加入一种可持续执行的小型冲泡或香气动作，只增加一个器具；
- 模块菜单款：同一茶底通过两到三种清楚模块变化，杯子仍属于同一产品家族。

### 8.2 R04 五类包装与价格方案

> Same warm-neutral tabletop, same three-quarter camera angle and same blank olive/cream visual zones. Produce separate high-fidelity mockups for: a daily takeaway cup; a compact portable gift box with internal support; a washable refillable container and return tray; three package sizes forming a clear price ladder without numbers; one drink-and-snack bundle that fits in one carrier. Show realistic stackability, handles, closures, cleaning and small-batch construction. No readable text or logo.

### 8.3 R05/R08 三条核心视觉路线

ImageGen 只生产相同触点的真实空白样机和氛围预览；正式 LOGO、IP 与品牌名必须由人工 SVG/HTML 叠加。

共同样机提示词：

> A controlled fair-comparison board photographed on one restrained contemporary tea-shop set inspired by warm paper, bamboo, light stone and muted olive accents. Show exactly the same five blank touchpoints at the same size and angle: narrow storefront sign, takeaway cup sleeve, paper carrier bag, circular phone avatar frame and monochrome receipt area. All placement zones are blank. Same lighting, material quality and spacing for every route; no existing brand, no readable text, no logo, no watermark.

三条路线的 SVG 设计 brief：

- **A 字标主导**：清楚横向中文品牌名；字体结构简洁，允许一条街巷/屋檐辅助线；在窄店招与 24 px 头像仍有明确读法；不能仿照参考图中的书法字或“三盏堂”。
- **B 符号主导**：原创、单色可用的抽象符号；从老街屋檐、茶叶展开或杯口负形中提炼，但不直接画“山+水+印章”；轮廓必须能在远距离与遮挡 35% 后识别。
- **C IP 主导**：原创、克制的茶饮角色；固定简洁轮廓，可由茶叶、茶杯或蒸汽形成一个核心特征；限定三种表情、两种动作；更像门店服务助手，不是儿童动画吉祥物。

### 8.4 R12 IP 物件

> One practical, small-batch producible object for walking and drinking around Tunxi Old Street: choose exactly one of cup-sleeve storage, tea-bag clip or portable cup carrier. Use the approved original IP only as a small functional cue on the object. Photorealistic product mockup with visible material seams, scale and carrying action. Restrained cream, tea-brown and muted olive palette. No table full of merchandise, no collectible-toy presentation, no readable text.

> Continue from the approved first-chapter image. Keep all identities and geometry. The main product, package structure and blank identity placement zones now form one coherent family across the sign and cup; the staff are still checking steps and training. Show progress through consistency, not luxury decoration.

### 第三章：服务与关系

> Continue from the approved second-chapter image. The peak flow is clearer, a returning resident and a visitor can both be served without blocking each other, and one small customer-sharing or feedback action occurs naturally. The shop feels lived-in and relational, with realistic wear and no staged celebration.

### 第四章：增长与取舍

> Continue from the approved third-chapter image at night. New and returning customers coexist, the team handles a manageable flow, and one growth-route prop is visible without dominating the shop. Preserve a small amount of operational pressure. Mature means the shop can keep its promise, not that every surface is full or every customer is cheering.
